What stands out

Key patterns reported by the analysis

2024 · Renewable crossover

Combined solar and wind generation reached 1,950 MWh, exceeding coal-based grid energy at 1,800 MWh for the first time.

Demand rose, emissions fell

Total energy increased from 3,200 to 4,050 MWh while reported CO₂ emissions declined by 53.4%.

Strong inverse relationship

The Pearson coefficient is −0.9988. This indicates a very strong inverse association, not proof of causation.

Data note: These values are the report's compiled dataset for an institutional and urban smart-grid cluster in Bengaluru. The report does not provide a downloadable raw-data source or independent audit trail. Treat the figures as project-study data, not official citywide statistics. Also, the correlation does not establish that renewable growth alone caused the emissions decline.
